The KateringKing Kitchen

The Culinary Masterclass

Engineered for Elegance. Crafted for Taste.

At KateringKing, we do not simply cook food; we curate gastronomic experiences. Our menus are a masterclass in flavor, blending the academic precision of modern hospitality with the rich, unyielding heritage of Indian culinary arts.

Crown Jewels of the KateringKing Kitchen

Exclusive masterpieces crafted for the discerning palate. These are the crown jewels of the KateringKing kitchen, requiring hours of slow-cooking, precision marinades, and expert technique.

I

The Royal Roasts & Cuts

Slow-Cooked Masterpieces

Dum Ki Raan

Whole leg of lamb slow-cooked for hours in a sealed vessel, marinated in a deep blend of spices — the centrepiece of any royal feast.

Kareli Roast (Lamb Shanks)

Tender lamb shanks deeply marinated and slow-roasted until the meat falls from the bone — bold, aromatic, and unforgettable.

Musbi (Dum Ka Bakra)

A whole goat prepared in the royal dum tradition — sealed, slow-cooked, and presented whole as the ultimate statement of culinary grandeur.

II

Artisanal Bites

Handcrafted with Precision

Muthi Ke Kebab

Hand-pressed minced meat kebabs finished over charcoal — each piece formed by hand, marinated overnight and grilled to perfection.

Warqi Samosa

A Hyderabadi classic — flaky, multi-layered pastry filled with seasoned meat, folded and fried until shatteringly crisp.

Lukmi (Mutton & Chicken)

The iconic square Hyderabadi pastry — a delicate, unleavened dough pocket filled with spiced minced meat and deep-fried to golden perfection.

III

The Centerpieces

Showstopper Dishes

Murgh Mussallam

A whole chicken marinated in rich spices and slow-cooked in the royal Mughal tradition — visually spectacular, impossibly tender.

Phattar Ka Gosht

An ancient Deccan technique — meat cooked directly on a scorching hot stone, sealing in all juices and creating a crust of extraordinary depth.

Dum Ke Chops

Succulent lamb chops marinated and sealed in a tight dum vessel, slow-cooked until every fibre is infused with aromatic spice.

Cold Fish Salads

Delicately spiced and elegantly composed — a refined contrast on the table, balancing the richness of the main feast with precision and grace.

Authentic Hyderabadi

A deeply researched homage to the kitchens of the Nizams. We execute these centuries-old recipes with flawless consistency, ensuring the authentic taste of Hyderabad is presented with premium sophistication.
